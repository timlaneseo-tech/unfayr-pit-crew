Sewer line repair and replacement

Sewage backing up into a tub or a gurgling toilet usually points to a problem in the main sewer line. We find the cause with a video camera, mark the exact depth and location of the damage and give you a written estimate before any digging starts.

Where the pipe allows it, trenchless repair lines the damaged section from the inside so your lawn, driveway and landscaping stay intact. When a pipe has collapsed or the grade is wrong we dig a short, targeted trench and replace only what has failed.

Older homes in the valley often have clay or cast iron lines that crack and let roots in. Catching a failing line early costs far less than an emergency dig after it collapses.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
