Boise plumbers who show up when they say they will

Serving Boise since 1998, Ridgeline Plumbing is a family-owned plumbing company that fixes leaks, clears drains, repairs sewer lines and replaces water heaters for homeowners and small businesses across the Treasure Valley. Our customers rate us 4.9 stars from 312 reviews, and most of our work comes from neighbours who tell their neighbours.

Call us for a dripping faucet or a flooded basement and you will talk to a person, not a menu. We book same-day visits whenever the schedule allows, and our emergency line is staffed around the clock for burst pipes and sewer backups.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.

Not sure what you need? Start with the service that sounds closest to your problem, or call and describe it in your own words. We will tell you honestly whether it is a quick fix or a bigger job.
