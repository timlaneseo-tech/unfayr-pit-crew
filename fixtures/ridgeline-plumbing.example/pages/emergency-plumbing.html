Emergency plumbing, day or night

Burst pipes, overflowing toilets, sewer backups and gas-fired water heaters that leak do not wait for business hours. Our emergency line is answered by a real person, and a technician is dispatched as soon as you call.

While you wait, shut off the main water valve, switch off the water heater and move valuables out of the way. Those two minutes can save a floor. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas company first.

Emergency visits are priced openly. We give you the rate before we leave the shop, stabilise the problem first and then talk through permanent repair options once the water is off and everyone is calm.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
