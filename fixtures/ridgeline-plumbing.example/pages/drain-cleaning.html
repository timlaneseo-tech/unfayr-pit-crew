Drain cleaning

A slow drain is a warning. Grease, hair, wipes and tree roots build up in the line until water has nowhere to go. We clear kitchen sinks, bathtubs, floor drains and main lines with cable machines and, when a line needs it, high-pressure water jetting.

After clearing a stubborn blockage we can run a camera through the pipe to see why it formed. A scrape, a sag or a root intrusion tells us whether the clog will come back, and we would rather show you the picture than guess.

Between visits, keep grease out of the sink, use strainers in showers and avoid flushing anything but toilet paper. Those three habits prevent most of the calls we get.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
