Plumber in Boise, Idaho

Boise is home base. Our shop is close enough that most calls from the North End, the Bench, Southeast Boise and Downtown reach you in a short drive. Many homes here were built decades ago and still have original galvanised or clay pipes that need attention.

Winter freezes, hard water and mature trees all shape the plumbing problems we see in the city. We know which neighbourhoods have shallow sewer lines, which have older cast iron and where the city water main changes pressure.

Call for any of our services, from a leaking faucet to a full sewer replacement. We are glad to look at an older house and tell you what is worth fixing now and what can wait.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
