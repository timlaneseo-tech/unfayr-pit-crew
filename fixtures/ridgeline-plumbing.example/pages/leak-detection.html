Leak detection and repair

A hidden leak wastes water, stains ceilings and feeds mould long before anyone sees a drip. If your water bill has crept up or you hear running water when everything is off, there is a good chance a pipe is leaking somewhere you cannot see.

We use acoustic listening equipment and thermal imaging to find the leak without tearing out walls. Once we know where it is we open a small access point, make the repair and patch the opening so the damage stays minimal.

Slab leaks, leaks behind tile and leaks in outdoor irrigation lines are all things we trace regularly. We also test pressure at the meter so you know the whole system is sound before we leave.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
