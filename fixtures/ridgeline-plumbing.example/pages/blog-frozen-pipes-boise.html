Why pipes freeze in Boise and how to prevent it

Pipes freeze when the temperature around them stays below freezing long enough for ice to form, and the pipes most at risk are the ones in unheated crawl spaces, garages and outside walls.

Before winter, disconnect garden hoses, shut off and drain outdoor faucets and insulate exposed lines. During a hard freeze, open cabinet doors under sinks and let a faucet drip so water keeps moving through the line.

If a pipe does freeze, thaw it slowly with a hair dryer starting at the faucet end, and never use an open flame. If you see a crack or hear water behind a wall, shut off the main and call for help right away.
