About Ridgeline Plumbing

Ridgeline Plumbing started in a single van and grew by doing honest work. Serving Boise since 1998, we are still owned and run by the family that started it, and we still answer the phone ourselves when we can.

Customers rate us 4.9 stars from 312 reviews. We read every one of them, the good and the critical, and use them to decide how to train new technicians and which habits to keep.

Every technician is licensed or working under a licensed plumber, is background checked and is trained to explain a problem plainly. We believe you should understand your own plumbing well enough to say yes or no with confidence.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
