What sewer line camera inspections cost

A sewer camera inspection feeds a small waterproof camera through the line and records what it sees. Price depends on access, the length of the line and whether the line needs cleaning first so the camera can see.

Inspections are most useful before you buy a house, when drains keep backing up, or when a tree has grown near the line. The video shows cracks, offsets, roots and low spots that no one could see from the surface.

Ask for the video and a written summary. A good inspection tells you what is wrong, how urgent it is and what your repair choices are, which makes the cost easy to justify.
