Water heater repair and replacement

No hot water on a cold morning is a problem you want solved today. We repair and replace tank and tankless water heaters, and we carry the parts for the most common brands so many repairs are done in a single visit.

Symptoms we see most often include a pilot light that will not stay lit, water that is lukewarm, rumbling or popping noises from the tank, and rusty water from the hot tap. A failed thermostat or heating element is usually a repair. A tank that is leaking from the bottom is nearly always a replacement.

When replacement is the sensible choice we explain the sizes and fuel types that suit your household, remove the old unit, haul it away and bring the installation up to current code. We flush the tank and check the anode rod so a new heater lasts as long as it should.

Every job starts with a plain conversation about what is wrong, what it will take to fix and what it will cost before we pick up a wrench. Our technicians arrive in marked trucks, wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up when they finish. If we find something else that needs attention we show you, explain the options and let you decide without pressure.

We stock the common parts on every truck, which means most repairs are finished on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered we tell you how long it will take and what to do in the meantime. Work carries a written warranty on both parts and labour, and we stand behind it.
